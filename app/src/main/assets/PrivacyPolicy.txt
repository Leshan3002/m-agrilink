M-AGRILINK PRIVACY POLICY
Last Updated: October 8, 2026

1. ACCOUNT IDENTITY & PERSISTENT SESSION RETENTION

All registration input they provide (Full Name, telephone registry
parameters such as email or phone number, and encrypted passwords) is
stored and retained locally on their mobile phone or tablet hardware
using Android Jetpack Preferences DataStore. These persistent identity
records are used solely to handle automatic bypass-logins on subsequent
boot launches to save their field time. They are never transmitted to
unauthorized third-party tracking metrics. Signing out clears their
session snapshot; deleting their data wipes it from the device.

2. LOCALIZED SENSITIVE INFORMATION HANDLERS

Image payloads they capture or retrieve via Shamba Scan and typed crop
information strings are compressed on-device and parsed by
high-precision generative vision models. Authentication uses a
production API key compiled from secure local device configuration at
build time; the key is never hardcoded in source, never written to
plaintext files, and never logged, so only a truncated fingerprint is
ever visible in diagnostics.

All returned diagnostic advisory briefs and regional climate parameters
are stored inside an offline-first local Room SQLite cache database to
prioritize data efficiency and prevent cellular bundle draining for
them. Cached rows remain on their device and are pruned automatically.

The application functions natively as a non-transactional informational
ledger space. The system creator bears zero legal or financial liability
for physical commodity transactions or off-platform logistical
scheduling arrangements taking place in their field. They must never pay
upfront deposits to transport operators before their produce is loaded
and weighed.

3. PERMISSIONS & DEVICE DATA

Camera access is used solely when they choose to scan crops. Location
access is used solely to fetch hyper-local weather for their field via
keyless public forecast endpoints. Microphone access is used solely when
they choose voice input. They may deny any permission; core offline
features continue to work for them.

4. CONTACT

For privacy questions they may reach the team via the in-app Contact
M-AgriLink Support action, which opens their mail client with a support
draft.
